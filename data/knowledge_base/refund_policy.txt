# ShopEase Refund Policy

ShopEase refunds are processed when an eligible order is cancelled, a return is approved, or a customer is eligible for a refund under the applicable ShopEase policy. Refund eligibility depends on the reason for the refund and the conditions of the relevant cancellation or return policy.

For prepaid orders, approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method used for the purchase. ShopEase normally processes refunds within 1–2 business days after the refund has been approved. The actual time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, a refund is not required when an order is cancelled before payment is collected. If a COD order is eligible for a refund after a completed purchase, ShopEase will provide the applicable refund through the supported refund process.

For damaged, defective, or wrong products, customers must report the issue within 7 days of delivery and provide the required photo or video proof. The claim will be verified before a refund or replacement is approved. If a refund is approved, it will be processed according to the ShopEase refund policy.

A refund may not be approved if the customer's request does not meet the applicable cancellation or return requirements, including required timelines, product-condition requirements, or verification requirements.
