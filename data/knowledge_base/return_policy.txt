# ShopEase Return Policy

Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving their order. For a normal return, the product must be unused and in its original condition. The product must also have its original packaging, original brand tag, and original label. Products with marks, scratches, or other signs of use may not be eligible for a normal return.

For damaged or defective products, customers must report the issue within 7 days of delivery. Photo or video proof of the damage or defect is mandatory, and the claim must be verified before the return is approved. Customers who receive the wrong product must also report the issue within 7 days and provide photo or video proof for verification.

Once a return is approved, ShopEase will arrange a return pickup from the customer's address. After the returned product is received and the applicable verification is completed, the customer may receive a refund or replacement depending on the case and product availability.
