ShopEase Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel their ShopEase order at any time before the order has been dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer available. However, if the customer no longer wants the order after dispatch, they may refuse to accept the package when the delivery agent attempts to deliver it.

For prepaid orders that are successfully cancelled before dispatch, ShopEase will process the refund to the original payment method. The refund will normally be processed within 1–2 business days. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, no refund is required when the order is cancelled because the customer has not made an advance payment.
